### How to vouch

Run it on your own machine and account, then cast what happened, including anything that failed. The checklist is in <RepoLink path="VOUCHER.md" />:

1. Note the commit you tested: `git rev-parse --short HEAD`.
2. Run the [Quickstart](../quickstart/) and record: provision time, time to sync, cost shown by Hetzner, server type and volume size.
3. Open the client from your node and confirm the feed loads.
4. Optional, **test account only**: add an app key and post one cast ([Your first cast](../first-cast/)). Record the cast hash and whether it appears in another client.
5. Run `teardown.sh` and confirm in the Hetzner console that nothing is left.
6. Cast your results publicly on Farcaster: the commit, timings, cost, cast hash (if any), teardown result, and anything that broke.

Never share your recovery phrase, your Hetzner token or your server's private details in the cast.
